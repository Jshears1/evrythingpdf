1. Information We Collect

EvrythingPDF processes PDF files locally in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit your files to any server. The only data we collect is standard web analytics (page views, anonymous usage statistics) to improve our service.

All PDF processing is performed client-side in your web browser. Your files are never uploaded to our servers. Once you close the browser tab, all file data is automatically cleared from memory.

We use minimal cookies for basic functionality and analytics. Third-party advertising cookies may be set by Google AdSense. By using our site you consent to cookie use.

For privacy inquiries, contact us via our contact page.
